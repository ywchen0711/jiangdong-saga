# Credits / 製作名單

江東霸業傳 Saga of Jiangdong is an original game. Its story, characters as written here, maps and code are original; it borrows the setting of the Three Kingdoms period (historical people and places are public history) and the feel of 16-bit army RPGs, but uses no names, maps, script, art, music or fonts from any commercial game.

All art and music come from openly licensed packs. Thank you to every artist below.

## Paper doll: Universal LPC Spritesheet Character Generator

Every general, soldier and townsperson is stacked at run time from Liberated Pixel Cup layers, taken from the [Universal LPC Spritesheet Character Generator](https://github.com/LiberatedPixelCup/Universal-LPC-Spritesheet-Character-Generator). The layers were cut down to the animations the game uses (walk, slash, thrust, shoot, spellcast, hurt, combat idle) by a build script, and are recoloured in the game with the generator's own palettes (assets/doll). ## Drawn in code for this game

- The world map: its outline, rivers, mountains and the ground painting are generated from a hand-made layout; the world-map sprites (mountains, hills, trees, villages, towns, castles, gates, caves, temples, ports, camps, the war junk) are original pixel art drawn by `tools/world_art.py` (assets/tiles/wobj.png).
- City walls, carpets, thrones, braziers, pillars, the lotus palace, window frames, battle backdrops and the title screen.
- Sound effects are synthesised in the browser.
