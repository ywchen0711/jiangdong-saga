# 江東霸業傳 Saga of Jiangdong

一款原創的三國軍團角色扮演遊戲，在瀏覽器裡就能玩。架空歷史：一夜之間，孫堅與孫策被神秘的「黑蓮教」毒殺。年輕的孫權逃出建業，在東海之濱救下四位從西方大海彼岸漂流而來的旅人——傑夫、艾琳、喬許、賈斯珀——然後一步步奪回江東，追查黑蓮教的線索，走遍天下。

An original Three Kingdoms army RPG that runs in the browser. Alternate history: in a single night the mysterious Black Lotus sect poisons Sun Jian and Sun Ce. Young Sun Quan flees Jianye, rescues four travellers shipwrecked from far beyond the western sea — Jeff, Erin, Josh and Jasper — wins back Jiangdong, and follows the sect's threads across the whole realm.

## 特色 Features

- **巨大的世界地圖** — 256×192 格的天下：長江、黃河、漢水、淮河，秦嶺與太行，西方的高原與沙漠，北方的長城與草原，東海的島嶼。六十多座城池、村落、關隘、港口、洞窟與寺廟，二十六個地區各有固定的敵軍強度（敵人永遠不會隨你的等級變強），在路上自然就能練功。小地圖、全圖、渡口、騎馬、戰船。
  **A huge world map** — 256×192 tiles of the Han lands: the Changjiang, Yellow, Han and Huai rivers, the Qinling and Taihang ranges, the western plateau and deserts, the Great Wall and the steppe, islands in the Eastern Sea. Over sixty cities, villages, passes, ports, caves and temples; twenty-six regions, each with its own fixed enemy strength (enemies never scale to you), so travel itself trains your army. Minimap, full map, ferries, horses and a warship.
- **軍團戰鬥** — 武將的兵力就是生命，士兵隨著損傷一個個倒下。攻擊、策略、道具、防禦、全軍突擊、撤退。兵種相剋（騎＞步＞弓＞騎），水戰時水軍佔優。
  **Army battles** — a general's troops are their life, and their little crowd of soldiers thins out as they fall. Attack, Tactic, Item, Defend, All-out Charge, Retreat. Horse beats foot beats bow beats horse; navies rule the water.
- **兵法** — 每一場仗都要動腦：兵種相剋（×1.3／×0.8，目標旁有▲▼）、陣形相剋（三組循環，克制方傷害+15%，戰鬥中可以「變陣」）、前後排（前排還在時刀槍打後排只剩六成，軍師與弓兵放後面；軍師必須出戰才能借出策略）、雙方士氣條（擊倒、會心、克制、打斷大招會提升；士氣崩潰的野戰敵軍會逃走，首領全倒則殘兵潰散）、策略成功率（智力、屬性、天候、地形，選單裡看得到）、帶回合數的狀態圖示（混亂、受困、昏睡、恐懼、燒傷、中毒、封策、詛咒、破甲、攻防速升降）。選單與戰鬥中的「兵法」頁解釋一切。
  **The art of war** — every fight asks for thought: the unit triangle (x1.3 / x0.8, with arrows by each target), formation counters (three circles of three, +15% for the winner, and a Formation order in battle), front and back rows (blades do 60% to the back while the front holds; keep the strategist and archers behind, and the strategist must fight to lend tactics), a morale bar per side (kills, crits, good matchups and broken-off moves raise it; a beaten field army runs, and when every leader falls the rest scatter), a visible success chance for every tactic (INT, element, weather, terrain), and statuses with turns shown as small icons. A Help page (兵法) in the menu and in battle explains it all.
- **首領戰** — 每位首領都有自己的機制與破解法，出戰前軍師會給提示，偵察可看清弱點：首領會預告大招並蓄力一回合（防禦、讓盾牆武將擋下、或用混亂／昏睡／封策打斷，有的怕水、怕火、怕雨），兵力降到六成、三成時改變戰法（增援、變陣、暴怒、吞丹、一回合兩動）。嚴白虎躲在親兵身後、孫暠的反計之陣、陳就的火船（東風會把火船吹回去）、黃祖的冷箭、潮音的江水、連環船與江霧、袁紹與替他療傷的霜鏡、華雄的一刀、董卓的鐵壁（落石或鋒矢陣才破得了）、季淵的離間、張任的伏兵與身法、焰無吸收火焰、霜鏡的鏡與冰、冥照的影兵、于吉的幻影與詛咒。
  **Leader battles** — every leader has a mechanic and an answer; advisers give a hint before the fight and scouting reveals the weak point. Leaders announce big moves a turn ahead (defend, let a shield-wall general take it, or break it off with confusion, sleep or Seal Ploys; some fear water, fire or rain) and change how they fight at 60% and 30% (reinforcements, a new formation, berserk rage, a black-lotus pill, two actions a round). Yan Baihu hides behind bodyguards, Sun Hao turns ploys back with a mirror stance, Chen Jiu sends fire ships (a wind blows them back), Huang Zu snipes, Chao Yin floods the field, a chained fleet hides in fog, Shuang Jing heals Yuan Shao, Hua Xiong cleaves, Dong Zhuo's Iron Wall breaks only under rocks or an Arrowhead charge, Ji Yuan sows discord, Zhang Ren ambushes and dodges blades, Yan Wu drinks fire, Shuang Jing switches mirror and ice, Ming Zhao's shadows explode, and Yu Ji conjures copies of your generals and curses your army.
- **戰場演出** — 五對五的武將分成三列排開，誰也不遮住誰（騎兵也一樣）；出手前會蓄勢、命中時有停頓與擊退、防禦時舉起光盾、施計時腳下發光、勝利時歡呼。士兵隨兵力減少一個個倒下淡出，挨打時會退縮；武將敗走是一段連續動作：踉蹌、跪倒、倒地揚起塵土再淡去，騎將落馬而馬匹奔逃。野戰的敵軍依地區更強（兵力、攻擊固定，不隨玩家等級），第二章以後常帶著會施計的軍師。
  **Battle staging** — five generals a side stand in three staggered columns so nobody covers anybody, cavalry included; attacks wind up, land with a hit-stop and knock-back, defenders raise a shield of light, casters glow, the army cheers on victory. Soldiers fall and fade one by one and flinch when hit; a routed general staggers, kneels, falls in a puff of dust and fades, and a mounted one is thrown while the horse bolts. Field armies are stronger by region (fixed troops and attack, never scaled to you) and from chapter two often bring a strategist who casts ploys.
- **三十六種策略** — 火計、大火計、烈火計、業火、水計、大水計、洪水、落石、山崩、雷擊、天雷、毒煙、混亂、挑撥、威嚇、破甲、封策、迷魂、埋伏、奇門、八門金鎖、鼓舞、堅守、疾風、治療、回復、大回復、復活、遁走、偵察、天變、呼風，以及傑夫的「火龍船」、艾琳的「觀星」。全軍共用的策略值由軍師的智力決定，戰鬥中不會自己恢復。
  **36 tactics** in families (fire, water, rock, thunder, status, support, healing, weather), sharing one strategy pool set by the army strategist's INT that does not refill during a battle.
- **十種陣形** — 方圓、魚鱗、鶴翼、鋒矢、長蛇、雁行、偃月、八卦、水軍、突騎。每種陣形改變誰會被攻擊、攻防速度與計策威力，有的需要條件；選單中有陣形圖。陣圖散落在天下各地。
  **10 formations**, each changing who gets hit, attack/defence/speed and tactic power, some with conditions, shown as diagrams. Manuals are hidden around the world.
- **三十種技能** — 連擊、突擊、鐵壁、神射、水戰、夜襲、激勵、醫術、反計、看破、威風、鬼謀、神算、護衛、不屈、一騎當千、鬼神、單騎、屯田、斥候、盾牆、工匠、觀星、妙手……
  **30 skills**, passive and active, some unique to famous generals.
- **五百名武將** — 一百三十多位歷史名將（能力依其史傳風格），加上以歷史姓氏與名字用字隨機生成的三百多位武將，分屬各方勢力。可以在酒館花錢或送禮招攬，也能在戰鬥後俘虜、招降。武將錄記錄你見過的每一個人。
  **500 generals**: 130+ famous people of the age plus seeded random ones from historical surnames and given-name characters. Hire them in taverns, win them over with gifts, or capture and persuade them after battle. The Who's Who records everyone you meet.
- **紙娃娃系統** — 每位武將的模樣（地圖上與戰場上）都在遊戲中由一層層圖片疊出來：身體膚色、頭、髮型、鬍鬚、衣服、鎧甲、頭盔、披風、武器、盾牌，坐騎時騎在馬上。換裝備，外表就跟著變；裝備畫面有即時預覽。五百位武將各有各的長相。
  **A paper doll**: every general is stacked at run time from layers — skin, head, hair, beard, clothes, armour, helm, cape, weapon, shield — and rides their horse. Equipment changes the look, with a live preview in the equip screen.
- 中英雙語、鍵盤／手把／觸控（手機直式與橫式皆可）、存檔三格與地圖上隨時存檔。
  Chinese and English, keyboard / gamepad / touch (phone portrait or landscape), three save slots and saving anywhere on the world map.

戰鬥中選「全軍突擊」可讓全軍自動攻擊，按住 B 取消——但面對首領，只會猛衝的軍隊通常會輸。
In battle, "All-out Charge" makes the whole army attack on its own; hold B to stop. Against a leader, an army that only charges will usually lose.

## 故事 The story

十個章節：血夜之後、周郎、還我江東、錦帆、荊襄風雲、長江怒濤、中原、長安迷霧、歸鄉、黑蓮。
Ten chapters: After the Night of Blood, The Young Master Zhou, Jiangdong Reclaimed, Brocade Sails, Storm over Jing and Xiang, Fury on the Changjiang, The Central Plains, Mist over Chang'an, Homecoming, The Black Lotus.

傑夫是造船匠和工程師（軍師人選，會造火船），艾琳是領航員和醫者（觀天象、治傷、水戰），喬許是大塊頭的盾兵（守護同伴），賈斯珀是最年輕的斥候和弓手（速度快、會偷東西、能發現地圖上的秘密）。
Jeff is a shipwright and engineer (a strategist who builds fire rafts), Erin a navigator and healer (stars, weather, wounds, the sea), Josh a big-hearted shield fighter (guards the others), and Jasper the youngest, a quick scout and archer (fast, light-fingered, finds secrets on the map).

## 執行 Running

All files are static. Serve the folder with any web server, for example `python -m http.server`, and open `index.html`. ES modules are imported with `?v=N` so a new version is fetched after an update.

`tools/doll_build.py` cuts the LPC layers (it needs a clone of the Universal LPC generator) and `tools/world_art.py` draws the world-map sprites.
