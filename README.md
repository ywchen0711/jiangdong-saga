# 江東霸業傳 Saga of Jiangdong

一款原創的三國軍團角色扮演遊戲，在瀏覽器裡就能玩。架空歷史：一夜之間，孫堅與孫策被神秘的「黑蓮教」毒殺。年輕的孫權逃出建業，在東海之濱救下被颱風吹離航線、從海東遠島漂流而來的一家人——父親傑夫、母親艾琳、十歲的喬許和八歲的賈斯珀——然後一步步奪回江東，追查黑蓮教的線索，走遍天下。

An original Three Kingdoms army RPG that runs in the browser. Alternate history: in a single night the mysterious Black Lotus sect poisons Sun Jian and Sun Ce. Young Sun Quan flees Jianye, rescues a family shipwrecked by a typhoon from an island far beyond the eastern sea — Jeff and Erin and their sons Josh (10) and Jasper (8) — wins back Jiangdong, and follows the sect's threads across the whole realm.

## 特色 Features

- **巨大的世界地圖** — 512×384 格的天下（從前的四倍大）：長江、黃河、漢水、淮河，秦嶺與太行，西方的高原與沙漠，北方的長城與草原，東海、南海與洞庭湖裡的島嶼。故事的地理不變，但城與城之間多了大片的田野、山林、海岸與海，旅行像一段真正的路程。九十八個地點，二十六個地區各有固定的敵軍強度（敵人永遠不會隨你的等級變強）。小地圖、三段縮放的全圖（地點依種類標示）、渡口、騎馬、渡船、戰船。
  **A huge world map** — 512×384 tiles of the Han lands, four times the old map: the Changjiang, Yellow, Han and Huai rivers, the Qinling and Taihang ranges, the western plateau and deserts, the Great Wall and the steppe, islands in the Eastern and Southern seas and in Lake Dongting. The story's geography is the same, but between its cities lie wide fields, forests, coasts and sea, so travel feels like a journey. 98 places; 26 regions, each with its own fixed enemy strength (enemies never scale to you). Minimap, a full map with three zooms and marks by kind, ferries, horses, boats and a warship.
- **三十三處秘境** — 每一處都是一張有人、有故事或秘密、有守敵與寶物的小地圖，有自己的樣子與音樂：
  - 洞窟：善卷洞（石灰岩）、潮音洞與珊瑚洞（海蝕洞）、銅官山礦（礦坑，鐵軌與礦車）、冰風洞（冰洞）、玄武岩窟（玄武岩）、夷洲的雲豹洞。
  - 村落：雲棲村（山村）、稻香村與桑田里（農村）、楓橋渡與孟津渡（渡口），都有客棧與道具店。
  - 島嶼（坐船才到得了）：螺洲村（海螺號角的故事）、鯨背島與望海燈塔、洞庭湖的君山島、珠崖港、蓬萊島的隱士。
  - 名山（之字形山道、半山的茶亭、山頂的景色）：天台山、泰山、峨眉山。
  - 瀑布（走進水簾，後面藏著洞穴）：香爐峰瀑布、壺口瀑布、白水瀑布。
  - 竹海、雲夢澤、子午谷；越王城遺址與殷墟（地宮）；赤眉寨與黃天寨（山寨）；長城上的烽火臺；驪山溫泉。
  有戰船之前，海鹽、東冶、長沙、合浦、臨淄的船夫會載你到島上。
  **33 places to explore** — each a small map with its people, a story or a secret, a guardian and something to find, with its own look and music: caves of limestone, sea rock, an old copper mine with rails and carts, ice and basalt; mountain, farming and ferry villages with inns and shops; islands reached by boat (the conch horn of Conch Isle, a lighthouse taken by pirates, Junshan in Lake Dongting, Pearl Cliff, the hermit of Penglai); three peaks climbed by switchbacks to a tea pavilion and a summit view; three waterfalls with a cave behind the water; the Bamboo Sea, the Yunmeng marsh and the Ziwu gorge; two ruins with crypts below; two bandit camps; a beacon tower on the Wall; a hot spring. Before you have a warship, boatmen in Haiyan, Dongye, Changsha, Hepu and Linzi take you to the islands.
- **自然升級** — 敵軍強度依地區固定，沿著故事的路線越往前越強；路上的秘境、守敵與首領都有經驗。照著故事走、順路探索，到每位首領面前時大約比他的軍隊低幾級（遠低於自己等級的敵人給的經驗很少，不會不小心練過頭）：首領要靠陣形、治療、計策打贏，不是靠練等級。等級上限 99。
  **Natural levelling** — enemy strength is fixed by region and rises along the story's road; the places to explore, their guardians and the leaders all give experience. Follow the story, explore what you pass, and you reach each leader a few levels below his army (he is beaten by formation, healing and ploys, not by grinding) (fights far below your level give little, so you do not over-level by accident). Level cap 99.
- **地形** — 地形對雙方一視同仁：平原與草原利騎兵；森林騎兵與弓兵受限、火計與埋伏更強；丘陵與山地利弓兵與落石；隘口只有前排打得到；淺灘騎兵難行、渡河的一方慢而弱；沼澤全軍變慢；沙漠、雪原各有影響；高地讓該方弓兵與防禦更強。野戰用遇敵那一格的地形，戰場背景、畫面右上與情報卡都顯示地形與它對每個人的影響。首領會挑好地方：張任守劍閣隘口與高地、焰無據山、顏良文醜隔河以待。
  **The ground** — the same for both sides: open ground and steppe favour horse; forests hamper horse and bows and feed fire and ambushes; hills and mountains favour bows and rockfalls; in a pass only the front rows can fight; a ford slows horse and whoever is wading; marsh slows everyone; desert and snow have their own effects; the side on high ground shoots and holds better. A random battle is fought on the ground of its tile; the backdrop, the top right of the screen and the info card show the ground and what it does to each general. Leaders choose good ground: Zhang Ren holds the pass and the heights of Jiange, Yan Wu waits on a mountain, Yan Liang and Wen Chou behind the river at Guandu.
- **攻城** — 十二場城池與關隘的戰鬥（烏程寨、皖城、秣陵關兩次、建業、夏口、襄陽、壽春、鄴城、虎牢關、潼關、郿塢）是真正的攻城戰：守軍站在城牆上（弓兵與防禦+25%），城門有自己的耐久，要用步兵、工匠、火計、火藥與投石車打破，騎兵只能撞門，步兵可以爬梯；守將會開門出城迎戰，拖太久援軍會到。開戰前的軍議可以偵察城防、斷糧道、夜襲、火燒城門、策反，每一項都是一般的手段（一場仗、火藥、金子、對的人）。
  **Sieges** — twelve battles at cities and gates (Wucheng, Wan, Moling twice, Jianye, Xiakou, Xiangyang, Shouchun, Ye, Hulao, Tong Gate, Mei) are real sieges: the defenders stand on the walls (archers and defence +25%), the gate has its own durability to break with foot, engineers, fire, gunpowder and the catapult, horse can only ram and foot can climb ladders; the defending leader may ride out, and a relief force comes if you are slow. Before the battle, a war council lets you scout the defences, cut the supply road, raid by night, burn the gate or win over a defender, each by ordinary means (a battle, gunpowder, gold, the right officer).
- **八人隊伍與營帳** — 最多八名武將隨軍（前列五人、後備三人），其餘在營帳等候：任何酒館或大本營的客棧都能換人、看情報、換裝備；營帳的武將保有裝備、分到兩成經驗，也能花錢訓練到隊伍平均等級減二。孫權與故事需要時的傑夫一家不會離隊。舊存檔超過八人時，等級最高的八人（前列優先）隨軍，其他人到營帳。
  **A party of eight and the camp** — at most eight generals travel (five in front, three in reserve); the rest wait at the camp: any tavern or the home city's inn swaps them, shows their card and changes their gear; generals in camp keep their gear, get a fifth of the experience, and can be trained for gold up to the party's average level less two. Sun Quan, and Jeff's family when the story needs them, never leave. Old saves with more than eight keep the eight best by level (front line first) and send the rest to camp.
- **軍團戰鬥** — 武將的兵力就是生命，士兵隨著損傷一個個倒下。攻擊、策略、道具、防禦、全軍突擊、撤退。兵種相剋（騎＞步＞弓＞騎），水戰時水軍佔優。
  **Army battles** — a general's troops are their life, and their little crowd of soldiers thins out as they fall. Attack, Tactic, Item, Defend, All-out Charge, Retreat. Horse beats foot beats bow beats horse; navies rule the water.
- **兵法** — 每一場仗都要動腦：兵種相剋（×1.3／×0.8，目標旁有▲▼）、陣形相剋（三組循環，克制方傷害+15%，戰鬥中可以「變陣」）、前後排（前排還在時刀槍打後排只剩六成，軍師與弓兵放後面；軍師必須出戰才能借出策略）、雙方士氣條（擊倒、會心、克制、打斷大招會提升；士氣崩潰的野戰敵軍會逃走，首領全倒則殘兵潰散）、策略成功率（智力、屬性、天候、地形，選單裡看得到）、帶回合數的狀態圖示（混亂、受困、昏睡、恐懼、燒傷、中毒、封策、詛咒、破甲、攻防速升降）。選單與戰鬥中的「兵法」頁解釋一切。
  **The art of war** — every fight asks for thought: the unit triangle (x1.3 / x0.8, with arrows by each target), formation counters (three circles of three, +15% for the winner, and a Formation order in battle), front and back rows (blades do 60% to the back while the front holds; keep the strategist and archers behind, and the strategist must fight to lend tactics), a morale bar per side (kills, crits, good matchups and broken-off moves raise it; a beaten field army runs, and when every leader falls the rest scatter), a visible success chance for every tactic (INT, element, weather, terrain), and statuses with turns shown as small icons. A Help page (兵法) in the menu and in battle explains it all.
- **首領戰** — 首領沒有特殊能力，和我們用同一套規則作戰：等級較高、穿著寶物、名將有自己的兩個特性（華雄的斬將、董卓的鐵壁與威壓、于吉的妖術……）、合理的陣形與兵種搭配，還有一位軍師與一個會用完的策略值（畫面右上「敵軍策略」）。每支軍隊排在最前面的主將（包括我們的）攻擊與防禦+15%；智力或統率85以上的敵將會看穿我軍陣形、被克制時變陣反制一次；首領戰中敵軍會集中攻擊兵力少的人與我軍軍師，兵力少時防禦，並且戰到最後一人；主將倒下則全軍士氣大跌。出戰前軍師會提醒該用什麼陣形、兵種與特性應戰，偵察能看清他們的特性、裝備與陣形。
  **Leader battles** — leaders have no special powers; they fight by the same rules as we do: a higher level, treasures, a famous general's own two traits (Hua Xiong's Duelist, Dong Zhuo's Iron Wall and Overawe, Yu Ji's sorcery...), a sensible formation and mix of troops, and a strategist with a strategy pool that runs out ("Enemy SP" at the top right). The general at the head of every army, ours included, is its commander, with +15% attack and defence; an enemy with INT or LEAD 85+ reads our formation and, unless his already beats ours, changes once (from the second round) to one that does; in a leader's battle the enemy works together, each round ganging up on the most worn-down of ours its blades can reach (a Taunt general draws them off), goes for our strategist, stands on guard when low and fights to the last; when a commander falls, his army's morale plunges. Advisers say which formation, troops and traits to answer with, and scouting shows their traits, gear and formation.
- **戰場演出** — 五對五的武將分成三列排開，誰也不遮住誰（騎兵也一樣）；出手前會蓄勢、命中時有停頓與擊退、防禦時舉起光盾、施計時腳下發光、勝利時歡呼。士兵隨兵力減少一個個倒下淡出，挨打時會退縮；武將敗走是一段連續動作：踉蹌、跪倒、倒地揚起塵土再淡去，騎將落馬而馬匹奔逃。野戰的敵軍依地區更強（兵力、攻擊固定，不隨玩家等級），第二章以後常帶著會施計的軍師。
  **Battle staging** — five generals a side stand in three staggered columns so nobody covers anybody, cavalry included; attacks wind up, land with a hit-stop and knock-back, defenders raise a shield of light, casters glow, the army cheers on victory. Soldiers fall and fade one by one and flinch when hit; a routed general staggers, kneels, falls in a puff of dust and fades, and a mounted one is thrown while the horse bolts. Field armies are stronger by region (fixed troops and attack, never scaled to you) and from chapter two often bring a strategist who casts ploys.
- **三十六種策略** — 火計、大火計、烈火計、業火、水計、大水計、洪水、落石、山崩、雷擊、天雷、毒煙、混亂、挑撥、威嚇、破甲、封策、迷魂、埋伏、奇門、八門金鎖、鼓舞、堅守、疾風、治療、回復、大回復、復活、遁走、偵察、天變、呼風，以及傑夫的「火龍船」、艾琳的「觀星」。全軍共用的策略值由軍師的智力決定，戰鬥中不會自己恢復。
  **36 tactics** in families (fire, water, rock, thunder, status, support, healing, weather), sharing one strategy pool set by the army strategist's INT that does not refill during a battle.
- **十一種陣形** — 方圓、魚鱗、鶴翼、鋒矢、長蛇、雁行、偃月、八卦、水軍、突騎，以及只能從試煉得到的三才陣。每種陣形改變誰會被攻擊、攻防速度與計策威力，有的需要條件；選單中有陣形圖。陣圖散落在天下各地（程普入夥時會傳授魚鱗陣）。
  **11 formations** (the Three Talents only from a trial), each changing who gets hit, attack/defence/speed and tactic power, some with conditions, shown as diagrams. Manuals are hidden around the world (Cheng Pu teaches the Fish Scale when the old generals join).
- **雙特性** — 五百名武將每人都有兩個被動特性，而且沒有兩人的組合相同。八十九種特性分成戰鬥、兵種、陣形、策略、狀態、輔助與傳說七類：連擊、會心、反擊、先登、背水、不屈、猛追、斬將、破軍、血戰、身法、挑釁、護主、威壓；騎射、槍陣、弓神、水師、鐵騎、山戰、擋箭；陣法家、鶴翼之才、先鋒、應變、破陣；火神、水攻、雷公、巨石、反間、遁甲、醫者、節約、連環、妖術；看破、冷靜、不眠、識破、百毒不侵、驅邪、毒計、堅韌；激勵、鼓吹、屯田、斥候、糧官、善退、收降、商才、識寶……也有少數猛進這類有代價的特性。名將的組合是親手挑的（關羽「武聖＋威壓」、諸葛亮「臥龍＋陣法家」、甘寧「水師＋奇襲」、黃忠「弓神＋背水」、呂布「飛將＋猛進」、周瑜「火神＋美周郎」、孫權「激勵＋碧眼兒」），十幾種傳說特性只屬於一個人；其他武將的組合依能力、兵種與勢力由種子決定，舊存檔也一樣。敵將同樣帶著特性，敵軍會用「挑釁」引開攻擊、用「護主」保護首領。特性觸發時，名字會浮現在武將頭上。兵法頁有全部特性的一覽（可分類瀏覽，列出持有的名將）；武將列表、武將錄、酒館與裝備畫面都看得到特性。
  **Two traits each** — every one of the 500 generals has two passive traits, and no two share a pair. 89 traits in seven families (combat, unit type, formation, tactics, status, support, legendary), each tied to the art of war: double strikes, keen eyes, ripostes, first-up charges, last stands, pursuit, duelists, armour breakers, bloodlust, taunts, loyal guards; horse archers, spear walls, bow saints, admirals, mountaineers, arrow wards; formation masters and breakers; fire lords, chain plots, turnabouts and dunjia; immunities; war drums, quartermasters, escape artists, persuaders, merchants and treasure eyes; and a few with a price, like Reckless. The famous have hand-picked pairs (Guan Yu: Saint of War + Overawe, Zhuge Liang: Sleeping Dragon + Formation Master, Gan Ning: Admiral + Surprise, Huang Zhong: Bow Saint + Last Stand, Lü Bu: Flying General + Reckless, Zhou Yu: Fire Lord + Handsome Zhou, Sun Quan: Inspire + Green-Eyed Lord), and over a dozen legendary traits belong to one person each; the rest get seeded pairs that suit their numbers, troops and side, the same in old saves. Enemies have traits too, and their AI taunts and guards its leaders. A trait's name rises over the general when it triggers; the Art of War has a glossary by family with who holds each one, and traits show in the generals list, the Who's Who, the tavern and the equip page.
- **戰場情報** — 戰鬥中選「情報」、按選單鍵，或直接點一下戰場上的武將，就會打開情報卡（不花回合，戰鬥會等你）：模樣、等級、兵種與對目前出手武將的相剋、兵力、武智統速與「原本→現在」的攻擊防禦（陣形、士氣、增減益、特性都算進去）、陣形對他的影響、剩餘回合的狀態、兩個特性、策略、裝備、弱點與抗性，主將、敵軍軍師的策略值與會不會看穿陣形。←→（或點左右兩側）翻看雙方每一個人。詳細抗性要偵察才看得到（顯示為？？？）；賈斯珀的「斥候」開戰就探明敵將，郭嘉的「鬼才」一開始就看穿全軍。
  **Battle intel** — Info in the command menu, the Menu key, or a tap on any general opens their card without using a turn (the battle waits): picture, level, unit type and the triangle against whoever is giving orders, troops, STR/INT/LEAD/SPD and attack and defence as base -> now (formation, morale, buffs and traits included), the formation's effect on them, statuses with turns left, both traits, tactics, gear, weak points and resistances, and whether they are a commander, the enemy strategist's points and whether they read formations. Left/right (or a tap on either side) pages through everyone on both sides. Exact resistances stay ??? until you scout; Jasper's Scouts reveal the enemy leader at the start, and Guo Jia's Ghost Talent sees through the whole army.
- **五百名武將** — 一百三十多位歷史名將（能力依其史傳風格），加上以歷史姓氏與名字用字隨機生成的三百多位武將，分屬各方勢力。可以在酒館花錢或送禮招攬，也能在戰鬥後俘虜、招降。武將錄記錄你見過的每一個人。
  **500 generals**: 130+ famous people of the age plus seeded random ones from historical surnames and given-name characters. Hire them in taverns, win them over with gifts, or capture and persuade them after battle. The Who's Who records everyone you meet.
- **紙娃娃系統** — 每位武將的模樣都在遊戲中由一層層圖片疊出來（身體膚色、頭、髮型、鬍鬚、衣服），再穿上七個裝備欄：武器、盾牌、鎧甲、頭盔、披風、坐騎、飾品。除了飾品，每一件都會改變武將在大地圖、城鎮、戰場與選單上的模樣（飾品也常帶來玉珮、項鍊、腰帶、護腕之類的小細節）：劍、雙刀、槍、戟、斧、鞭錘、弓、弩、羽扇、法杖各有樣子；布衣、皮甲、鎖子甲、札甲、魚鱗甲、明光鎧、藤甲、水靠；頭巾、皮帽、鐵盔、纓盔、鳳翅盔、獅子盔、綸巾、道冠、羌族頭巾；各色披風、盾牌與十一種毛色的馬。青銅、鐵灰、鋼藍、白銀、黃金分出等級，傳說級的寶物帶著一圈光芒。裝備畫面有一個會原地轉身的大型預覽，旁邊是戰鬥姿勢（有馬就騎在馬上），試穿背包或商店裡的任何東西都能先看樣子與能力的增減；武將列表與武將錄也顯示每個人現在的打扮。
  **A paper doll you can see**: every general is stacked at run time from layers (skin, head, hair, beard, clothes) and then wears seven slots: weapon, shield, armour, helm, cape, mount and accessory. ## 執行 Running

All files are static. Serve the folder with any web server, for example `python -m http.server`, and open `index.html`. ES modules are imported with `?v=N` so a new version is fetched after an update.

The places to explore are built in `js/explore.js` (maps, people, guardians, ferries, the summit view); the ground and sieges are in `js/battle.js` (TERRAIN, the gate) with the leaders' ground and the war council in `js/story.js` (BOSS_GROUND, warCouncil). `tools/doll_build.py` cuts the LPC layers (it needs a clone of the Universal LPC generator), `tools/item_icons.py` packs the Ninja Adventure item pictures used for icons, and `tools/world_art.py` draws the world-map sprites. The treasures are a table in `js/treasures.js`; the trials (side quests) are in `js/sidequests.js` with their boss kits in `js/bosses.js`.
